Platform Architecture

The Dot Process Engine.

A unified workflow designed for scale. Explore how our proprietary matching and rigorous quality controls deliver perfection every time.

Step 1

Unified Platform Entry

Clients seamlessly submit specific requirements (Event Type, Location, Budget, Style) directly into our centralized data pipeline via search, social, or referral funnels.

Step 2

Algorithmic Availability Check

Our proprietary system instantly queries our vetted database, filtering top-tier creative partners against hyper-specific local availability, skill category, and exact budget parameters.

Step 3

Human-in-the-Loop Consultation

The internal DOT team initiates direct contact via preferred channels (WhatsApp/Call/Email). We finalize briefs, understand nuanced creative visions, and confirm optimized package routing.

Step 4

Secure Booking & Dedicated RM

Post-payment confirmation, the booking is secured. An exclusive Relationship Manager (RM) is automatically assigned to oversee total timeline coordination and stress-free project communication.

Step 5

On-ground Execution

Vetted creative partners arrive on-site, executing comprehensive photography and videography flawlessly according to your dedicated RM's brief.

Step 6

Internal QA Pipeline

Rigorous internal review of all raw outputs. Our specialized editing team steps in to color-grade and ensure strict brand quality standards are met.

Step 7

Cloud Delivery

Delivery of high-resolution, perfected assets via secure Drive links alongside custom physical album options, within our promised timeline.

Step 8

CRM Integration & Feedback

Automated post-delivery check-ins collect vital feedback, updating our CRM database and inviting you to our scalable referral program.
